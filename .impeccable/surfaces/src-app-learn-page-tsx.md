# Learner workspace

Scope: Learner home and learning routes; public, authentication, and family surfaces inherit the book-first system while keeping their own task hierarchy.
Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: Learners 15+, including secondary, college, and university students, continue lessons, choose what to study next, and understand progress. Ages 15–17 remain guardian-supported; 18+ learners use self-managed accounts. Ages 24+ may access the product, but the experience must not claim age-tailored content beyond the verified curriculum range.
Constraints: Preserve working lessons, quizzes, study guides, focus tools, progress, accessibility, privacy, consent, safeguarding, and account behavior. Keep the book theme. Remove AI terminology and AI-styled visuals from user-facing surfaces.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A learner should find the next useful study step immediately; the category-default stack of generic dashboard cards gives way to a considered reading workspace.

OWN-WORLD: The Annotated Reader uses warm uncoated-paper surfaces, deep book-cloth green, restrained rust and brass accents, editorial serif headings, and highly legible sans-serif controls. Margins, chapter cues, and page references carry real navigation or context, never decoration.

STORY: A learner arrives, understands what is ready to continue, chooses a lesson or study tool, and can review progress without pressure or judgement.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep familiar app navigation at the left on wide screens and native-feeling compact navigation on phones. Give the current lesson the largest area, with its title, subject, clear progress and one primary action; place a slim chapter index and useful study note beside it. Follow with a readable list of other study tasks.

FORM: The Annotated Reader, the highest-ranked grounded direction and the user's selected option from direction seed a1e2564d. Its form is a working learner dashboard, not a literal book mock-up.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
